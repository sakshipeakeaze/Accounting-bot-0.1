# Accounting Frameworks and Standards

## 1. Compliance Standards
* **UK GAAP (FRS 102):** Most client annual accounts are prepared under FRS 102 Section 1A for small entities.
* **IFRS Alignment:** For larger international clients, we utilize IFRS-compliant reporting for consolidated group statements.

## 2. Revenue Recognition Policy
* **Fixed-Fee Retainers:** Revenue for recurring bookkeeping is recognized monthly as services are rendered.
* **Project-Based Audits:** Recognized on a "Percentage of Completion" (PoC) basis.
* **Peakeaze IT Integration:** IT consultancy revenue is recognized upon milestone delivery (e.g., successful ERP bridge deployment).

## 3. Departmental Specializations
We provide dedicated teams for varied sectors, including:
* **Health & Pharmacy:** Specialized pharmacist VAT schemes.
* **Construction (CIS):** Management of Construction Industry Scheme filings.
* **IT Contractors:** High-volume, low-complexity accounting for freelancers.
