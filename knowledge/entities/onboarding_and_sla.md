# Service Level Agreements (SLA) and Onboarding

## 1. Turnaround Time (TAT) Targets
* **Weekly Bookkeeping:** 24-hour update cycle.
* **VAT Returns:** Finalization within 5 business days of document receipt.
* **Payroll:** Guaranteed 48-hour delivery before pay-date.
* **Annual Accounts:** Draft accounts within 10 business days post-audit-info receipt.

## 2. Onboarding Requirements (KYC/AML)
Every new client must undergo a strict onboarding process via our portal:
* **Identity Proof:** Passports/IDs for all directors.
* **KYC Portal:** Automated screening against sanction lists using 'Veriff' or 'Creditsafe'.
* **Engagement Letter:** Digital signature required on the Master Services Agreement (MSA).

## 3. Escalation Matrix
* **Level 1:** Dedicated Client Manager (4-hour response).
* **Level 2:** Operations Head (8-hour resolution).
* **Level 3:** Directors (Avantika Rathi / Nikhil Narang) for high-impact disputes.
