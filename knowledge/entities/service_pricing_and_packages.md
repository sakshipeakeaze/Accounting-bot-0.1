# Pricing and Engagement Models

## 1. Managed Full-Time Employee (MFTE) Model
Designed for larger accounting practices wanting a dedicated offshore team.
* **Junior Accountant (FTE):** £1,500 – £2,000 / month.
* **Senior/Manager (FTE):** £2,500 – £3,500 / month.
* **Scope:** 160 hours/month, dedicated supervisor, and UK-compliant training.
